El Paso Complex: Hospitality in Buea

Big M Lodging Services is the hospitality arm of Big M Business Group Ltd. Through El Paso Complex, we offer comfortable accommodation, restaurant dining and the vibrant Bukaro bar, all under one roof in Molyko, Buea.

Whether you are visiting Buea for business, a family occasion, or simply looking for a welcoming place to stay and relax, El Paso Complex provides a complete hospitality experience in one convenient location.

What We Offer

Accommodation
Hotel rooms, guest studios, VIP rooms, apartments and a fully equipped event center.
Restaurant
Freshly prepared local and continental dishes served in a relaxed setting.
Bukaro and Bar
A welcoming bar serving a wide range of drinks in a lively, comfortable atmosphere.

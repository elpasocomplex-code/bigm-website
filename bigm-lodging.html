El Paso Complex — Hospitality in Buea

Big M Lodging Services is the hospitality arm of Big M Business Group Ltd. Through El Paso Complex, we offer comfortable accommodation, restaurant dining and the vibrant Bukaro bar — all under one roof in Molyko, Buea.

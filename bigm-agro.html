Building a Professional Livestock Operation

Big M AgroVentures is the agricultural arm of Big M Business Group Ltd. Our current focus is on commercial pig breeding and production. This is a sector with strong, growing demand across Cameroon and clear opportunities for responsible, well-managed expansion.

We believe agriculture should be run like a business, with proper planning, responsible farm management and a long-term view. Our goal is to build a professionally managed livestock operation that supplies quality products, creates employment and contributes to national food supply.

Looking Ahead

Our long-term strategy is to build a scalable, professionally managed livestock production operation. As the business grows, we plan to expand capacity, improve facilities and develop partnerships with buyers, distributors and suppliers across the country.

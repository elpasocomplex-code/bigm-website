How It Works

A simple, transparent process. You buy your items in the USA — we collect, load and ship them to Cameroon.

1
Buy Your Items in the USA
Shop in the USA as you normally would — or ask us to help you buy on your behalf. Cars, electronics, mattresses, sofas, barrels, anything.
2
Send to Our Virginia Collection Point
Ship or drop off your items at our collaborator's location in Virginia. We also arrange pickups in nearby areas.
3
We Load Into a 40 ft Container
Once enough items are gathered, everything is carefully loaded into a 40 ft container — ready for shipping.
4
Delivered in Cameroon
Your goods are shipped to Cameroon and delivered to you. We keep you updated along the way.

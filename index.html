Our Story

Big M Business Group didn't start as a group. It started with a small hospitality business in Buea.

That business taught us how difficult it is to grow something real in Cameroon. Capital is hard to find. Systems are hard to build. Imports are complicated. And nobody explains any of it.

So we built every tool we wished we'd had. A way to move people and goods. A way to bring things in from abroad. A way to fund the next step. A way to learn what nobody was teaching. A way to run a business with real numbers. A way to produce and grow at scale. A way to hold onto what you've built.

Over time, those tools became eight businesses. Today, we offer them to other Cameroonians — through 8 subsidiaries that answer the questions we once had ourselves.

We build businesses. Then we help you build yours.

Our Mission

To close the gap between ambition and execution for Cameroonians — by giving them the funding, tools, systems and knowledge that took us years to build ourselves.

Our Vision

A Cameroon where anyone with ambition can build a real business — because the pieces are finally in one place, and someone is willing to show them how.

Our Values

Practical over theoretical. Honest over polished. Long-term over quick wins. And proof over promises — we built our own businesses first, so we speak from experience, not theory.

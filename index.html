Our Story

Big M Business Group did not start as a group.

It started with a small hospitality business in Buea. That business taught us how difficult it is to grow something real in Cameroon. Capital is hard to find. Systems are hard to build. Imports are complicated. And nobody explains any of it.

So we built every tool we wished we had. A way to move people and goods. A way to bring things in from abroad. A way to fund the next step. A way to learn what nobody was teaching. A way to run a business with real numbers. A way to produce and grow at scale. A way to hold on to what you have built.

Over time, those tools became eight businesses. Today, we offer them to other Cameroonians through eight subsidiaries that answer the questions we once had ourselves.

We build businesses. Then we help you build yours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often. If yours is not here, send us a message.

Which of your businesses is right for me?+
Tell us what you are trying to do and where you are starting from. If you need capital, we point you to Big M Loan Services. If you want to import goods, Big M Brokante. If you want to run your business with real numbers, Big M Business Solutions. If you are not sure, contact us on WhatsApp and we will guide you.
Can I start a business in Cameroon if I live abroad?+
Yes. Several of our services are designed with the diaspora in mind, including shipping from the USA through Big M Brokante and remote support through WhatsApp for enquiries and applications. You do not have to be physically present to get started.
How do I know a loan application will be approved?+
We cannot guarantee approval. Every application is reviewed on its own merits by the Big M Loan Services team. Please note that a processing fee of 10,000 Frs is charged for the loan application form. This fee covers the cost of reviewing and processing your file. Once your application is submitted, we will contact you with the outcome and next steps clearly.
What kinds of items can you ship from the USA?+
If it fits in a 40 ft container, we can ship it. Popular items include cars, barrels, mattresses, boxes, sofas, electronics and household goods. We also arrange pickups in Virginia, Maryland, Stafford, Virginia Beach, Richmond and Woodbridge. Contact Big M Brokante for a quote.
Can you install the sales and stock system in my shop or bar?+
Yes. Big M Business Solutions installs digital menus and sales and stock control systems for bars, restaurants, provision stores, supermarkets, pharmacies, fashion shops, bookshops and electronics stores. Installation, setup and staff training are all included. Contact us on WhatsApp to arrange a visit.
How do I contact the right business directly?+
The quickest way is WhatsApp on 678 639 128. You can also reach us by email at bigmbusinessgroup@gmail.com. Each subsidiary page also has its own dedicated link, whether that is a Microsoft Form, our Selar store or our El Paso digital menu.

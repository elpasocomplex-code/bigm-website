About Big M Business Group

A diversified group building businesses that serve people and create lasting value.

Big M Business Group Ltd is the parent organisation overseeing a growing portfolio of business ventures across Cameroon. Our vision is to build a strong portfolio of sustainable businesses that empower individuals, serve communities and contribute to economic development. Through our subsidiaries, we transform ideas into practical services and scalable business opportunities.

Our Mission

To build and manage sustainable businesses that deliver practical solutions, create opportunities and improve the everyday lives of the people and communities we serve.

Our Vision

To become a leading diversified business group in Cameroon — recognised for integrity, innovation, and long-term value creation across every sector we operate in.

Our Values

Integrity. Excellence. Innovation. Service. Long-term thinking. We hold ourselves to high standards in every business, every transaction and every relationship.
